The History and Standards of Distance Measurement

The human measurement of length dates back millennia to anatomical benchmarks such as the cubit (the length of the forearm from elbow to fingertip), the foot, and the pace. In 1959, the International Yard and Pound Agreement formally standardized imperial units against metric standards, defining 1 yard as exactly 0.9144 meters and 1 inch as exactly 25.4 millimeters.

Practical Conversion Shortcuts

  • Inches to Centimeters: Multiply inches by 2.5 (e.g. 6 inches × 2.5 = ~15 cm; exact is 15.24 cm).
  • Meters to Feet: Multiply meters by 3.3 (e.g. 10 meters × 3.3 = ~33 feet; exact is 32.81 ft).
  • Miles to Kilometers: Multiply miles by 1.6 (e.g. 50 miles × 1.6 = 80 km; exact is 80.47 km).
