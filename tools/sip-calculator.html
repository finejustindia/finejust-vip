How Systematic Investment Plans (SIP) Generate Compound Wealth

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows individuals to invest fixed sums regularly into mutual funds, index exchange-traded funds (ETFs), or equity portfolios. By combining Rupee/Dollar Cost Averaging and the exponential mathematics of monthly compounding, SIPs maximize wealth creation over long-term market cycles.

The Future Value (FV) Annuity Compounding Formula

M = P × [((1 + i)n - 1) / i] × (1 + i)

Where:
• M = Future Maturity Wealth Value
• P = Monthly SIP Contribution
• i = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
• n = Total Number of Monthly Installments (Years × 12)
