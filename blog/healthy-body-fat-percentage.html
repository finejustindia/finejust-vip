1. What is Body Fat Percentage?

3. The U.S. Navy Circumference Formula

Developed by the Naval Health Research Center, the U.S. Navy Circumference Method estimates body fat within ~3% accuracy of medical DEXA scans using common body circumference measurements:

Men: %Fat = 495 / (1.0324 - 0.19077 × log10(waist - neck) + 0.15456 × log10(height)) - 450

Women: %Fat = 495 / (1.29579 - 0.35004 × log10(waist + hip - neck) + 0.22100 × log10(height)) - 450

4. Comparing DEXA, Calipers, BIA & Navy Method

  • DEXA Scan: Medical gold standard measuring bone density, lean mass, and visceral fat (~1-2% margin of error).
  • U.S. Navy Method: Best free home assessment requiring only a tape measure (~3% error).
  • Bioelectrical Impedance (BIA Scales): Highly sensitive to hydration status, giving erratic day-to-day readings (~5-8% error).
  • Skinfold Calipers: Reliable when performed by an experienced technician (~3-4% error).

Frequently Asked Questions (10)

Body fat percentage measures the actual ratio of adipose fat to lean muscle mass, whereas BMI only evaluates gross body weight.

A fitness range of 14% to 17% and an athletic range of 6% to 13% are ideal for most adult men.

A fitness range of 21% to 24% and an athletic range of 14% to 20% are ideal for adult women.

Women require higher essential body fat (10%-13% vs 2%-5% for men) for estrogen production, reproductive health, and mammary gland function.

When measured carefully with consistent tape tension, the U.S. Navy method correlates within 3% to 4% of clinical DEXA scans.

For men, measure horizontally across the navel (belly button); for women, measure at the narrowest point of the natural waist.

Visceral fat is adipose tissue surrounding internal abdominal organs that increases risk for type 2 diabetes and heart disease.

Losing 0.5% to 1.0% of total body weight (approx. 1 to 2 lbs of fat) per week preserves lean muscle mass.

Smart bio-impedance scales can fluctuate wildly based on hydration, salt intake, and recent meals, making them less reliable than circumference measurements.
