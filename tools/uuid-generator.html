Understanding Universally Unique Identifiers (UUID v4)

A UUID (Universally Unique Identifier) is a 128-bit label used for information in computer systems. Standard UUIDs follow the 8-4-4-4-12 hexadecimal character representation: xxxxxxxx-xxxx-4xxx-yxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx where 4 represents version 4 and y encodes the variant (8, 9, a, or b).
