Cookie Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026 • Clear disclosure on how cookies, local storage, and third-party advertising technologies operate on Finejust.vip.

Cookie Overview & Privacy Summary
  • Zero User Input Cookies: We do NOT use cookies to monitor or save the numbers, text, or formulas you enter in our calculators.
  • Local Theme Storage: We use your browser's local storage solely to remember whether you selected Dark Mode or Light Mode.
  • Google AdSense Advertising Cookies: Google and certified third-party vendors use cookies (such as DART cookies) to serve non-intrusive ads.
  • Easy Opt-Out: You can disable all cookies in your browser settings at any time without breaking tool functionality.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used by web platforms to make websites work efficiently, enhance user navigation, and deliver aggregated analytics and relevant advertising.

Local Storage (HTML5 localStorage) is a browser technology that allows web applications to store key-value data locally inside your browser sandbox without transmitting that data across the network to web servers on every HTTP request.

2. How Finejust.vip Uses Cookies & Data Minimization

At Finejust.vip, we practice strict data minimization. We believe online utilities should be instantaneous, helpful, and completely respectful of your privacy.

When you use our suite of over 30+ calculators and converters—such as the Percentage Calculator, BMI Calculator, EMI Calculator, GST Calculator, or Password Generator—all computation runs locally in your browser memory via Vanilla JavaScript. We do not use tracking cookies to spy on your calculations, passwords, or personal metrics.

3. Essential & Preference Storage (Local Theme Preference)

We utilize your browser's localStorage for exactly one preference setting:

  • Key: finejust_theme
  • Stored Values: 'dark' or 'light'
  • Purpose: Remembers your preferred color theme so your screen does not flicker when navigating between different tool pages.
  • Transmission: Stored 100% locally on your machine; never sent to our servers.

4. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

To maintain Finejust.vip as a 100% free web utility with no paywalls or mandatory subscriptions, we display third-party advertisements powered primarily by Google AdSense.

Google DART Cookie Disclosure:

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on Finejust.vip. Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to our users based on their visits to Finejust.vip and other sites across the internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings portal.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements. These cookies measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and personalize the advertising content that you see. Finejust.vip has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

6. How to Manage, Block, or Delete Cookies in Your Browser

You have complete control over how your browser handles cookies. You can configure your browser to reject all cookies, accept only first-party cookies, or notify you each time a cookie is offered.

  • Google Chrome: Open Menu → Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Open Menu → Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Open Settings → Safari → Privacy & Security → Block All Cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Open Menu → Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

To opt out of third-party behavioral advertising network-wide, visit:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) Opt-Out
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) WebChoices
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA)
