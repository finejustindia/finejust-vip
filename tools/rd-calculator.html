How Recurring Deposit (RD) Monthly Savings Grow

A Recurring Deposit (RD) is a structured banking savings plan where you invest a fixed monthly installment over a chosen term. Unlike a lump-sum Fixed Deposit, an RD enables disciplined regular monthly savings while earning the same high guaranteed interest rates as FDs.

Standard Banking Formula for Recurring Deposits

M = P × [(1 + i)n - 1] / [1 - (1 + i)-1/3]

Where quarterly compounding is applied across monthly installments according to standard Indian and international commercial bank regulations.
