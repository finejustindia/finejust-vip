How Bank Fixed Deposit (FD) Compounding Works

A Fixed Deposit (FD) or Term Deposit is a guaranteed financial instrument offered by commercial banks and financial institutions. You lock in a fixed principal sum for a predetermined period at a guaranteed interest rate.

The Standard Compound Interest Formula for FDs

A = P × (1 + r / n)(n × t)

Where:
• A = Maturity Amount Payable at End of Tenure
• P = Principal Deposit Sum
• r = Nominal Annual Interest Rate (in decimal form)
• n = Number of Compounding Intervals Per Year (4 for Quarterly)
• t = Total Number of Years
