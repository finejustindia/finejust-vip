How Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) & Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) Work

Your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) represents the total amount of calories burned each day through basal bodily functions (BMR), physical activity, and food digestion.

Mifflin-St Jeor Equation:
Men: BMR = (10 × Weight kg) + (6.25 × Height cm) - (5 × Age) + 5
Women: BMR = (10 × Weight kg) + (6.25 × Height cm) - (5 × Age) - 161

TDEE = BMR × Physical Activity Multiplier
