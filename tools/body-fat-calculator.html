How the U.S. Navy Body Fat Formula Works

The U.S. Navy Body Fat Method is a validated anthropometric formula estimating total adipose tissue percentage from circumference measurements of the neck, waist, height, and hips.

Men: % Fat = 495 / [1.0324 - 0.19077(log10(waist - neck)) + 0.15456(log10(height))] - 450

Women: % Fat = 495 / [1.29579 - 0.35004(log10(waist + hip - neck)) + 0.22100(log10(height))] - 450
