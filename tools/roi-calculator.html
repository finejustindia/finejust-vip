How Return on Investment (ROI) and Annualized CAGR are Calculated

Return on Investment (ROI) evaluates the profitability and economic efficiency of an asset or business venture relative to its original capital cost.

ROI (%) = [(Final Value - Initial Cost) / Initial Cost] × 100

Annualized ROI (CAGR) = [(Final Value / Initial Cost)(1 / Years) - 1] × 100
