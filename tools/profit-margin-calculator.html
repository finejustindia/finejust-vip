Understanding Gross Profit Margin vs Markup Percentage

While Gross Margin and Markup describe the same economic profit, their calculation bases differ:

Gross Profit = Selling Price - Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

Gross Profit Margin (%) = (Gross Profit / Selling Price) × 100
Markup Percentage (%) = (Gross Profit / Cost) × 100
