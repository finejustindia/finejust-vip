The Physics and Practical Applications of Velocity Conversion

Speed is defined as the magnitude of change in position per unit of time (a scalar quantity), whereas velocity incorporates both magnitude and directional vector. Converting between different velocity standards is critical in transportation, automotive engineering, aerospace navigation, meteorological storm tracking, and athletics.

Standard Conversion Ratios (Standardized to Meters per Second)

• 1 Meter per Second (m/s) = 3.6000 Kilometers per Hour (km/h)
• 1 Mile per Hour (mph) = 0.44704 Meters per Second (m/s) = 1.609344 km/h
• 1 Knot (kn) = 1 Nautical Mile / hr = 0.514444 m/s = 1.852 km/h
• 1 Foot per Second (ft/s) = 0.3048 m/s = 1.09728 km/h
• Mach 1 (Sea Level, 20 °C) = 343.0 m/s = 1,234.8 km/h = 767.269 mph
