Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 15, 2026 • Last Reviewed: August 15, 2026

Privacy Highlights & Data Minimization Guarantee
  • 100% Client-Side Computation: All calculator formulas, cryptographic encoders, text counters, and unit conversions execute locally in your web browser. Your inputs are never sent to our servers.
  • No Account Required: We never ask for registration, credit cards, or phone numbers.
  • Zero Personal Data Sales: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data under CCPA/CPRA.
  • Google AdSense Compliance: Transparent disclosures regarding third-party advertising cookies and simple opt-out controls.

1. Introduction & Scope

Welcome to Finejust.vip (referred to as "we", "our", "us", or the "Platform"), accessible via https://finejust.vip. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to safeguarding it through strict adherence to international data privacy principles.

This Privacy Policy explains the categories of information we collect (and deliberately do not collect), how third-party services operate on our platform, how cookies are utilized, and your legal rights under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Client-Side Tool Processing Architecture (Zero Data Storage)

The foundational design of Finejust.vip is built upon Client-Side Execution. When you use our utilities—including but not limited to the Percentage Calculator, BMI Calculator, EMI Calculator, GST Calculator, Password Generator, Word Counter, and QR Code Generator:

  • All mathematical formulas, text manipulations, and cryptographic computations run directly within your device's browser memory (RAM) via Vanilla JavaScript.
  • Your numbers, financial amounts, body measurements, passphrases, and text drafts are never transmitted across the network to our web servers or any external database.
  • When you close or refresh your browser tab, all calculated values and temporary session inputs are instantly wiped from your memory.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

To keep our tools, calculators, and educational guides 100% free for users worldwide, Finejust.vip partners with third-party advertising vendors, predominantly Google AdSense. Google and other advertising networks use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our platform and other websites on the internet.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie Notice:

Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to Finejust.vip and/or other sites on the Internet. Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google's official Google Ads Settings.

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Finejust.vip. These technologies automatically receive your IP address when this occurs to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see.

Finejust.vip has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers. We encourage you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. You can review how Google handles data in its advertising partner network by visiting: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. How Users Can Opt Out of Personalized Ads

You have the full right and technical capability to opt out of interest-based or personalized advertising:

  • Google Ads Opt-Out: Visit the Google Ads Settings Portal to adjust your ad preferences or turn off personalization.
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): Visit https://optout.networkadvertising.org to opt out of participating third-party ad networks.
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA): Visit the DAA WebChoices Browser Tool.
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance (EDAA): If you are located in the European Union, you can exercise ad choices at https://www.youronlinechoices.eu.

5. Log Files & Web Hosting Diagnostics

Finejust.vip follows standard web hosting operational procedures by utilizing server log files. These files log visitors when they access web pages. The information collected by log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized/aggregated)
  • Browser type and browser engine version
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamps of request headers
  • Referring / exit pages and requested URL paths
  • Number of clicks and HTTP response status codes

These logs are maintained strictly for cybersecurity defense, mitigating Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, diagnosing routing errors, and analyzing overall aggregate traffic patterns. This technical data is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

6. Cookie Policy & Local Storage Management

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Finejust.vip minimizes cookie usage:

Cookie / Storage Type Provider Purpose & Retention
localStorage ('finejust_theme') Finejust.vip Remembers your Dark or Light theme toggle selection across page visits. Stored locally until cleared.
Advertising Cookies (DART) Google AdSense / Partners Serves non-intrusive, relevant advertisements and prevents duplicate ad impressions.
Diagnostic Cookies Cloudflare / CDN Infrastructure Ensures DDoS protection, SSL security verification, and bot management.

You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. To manage or delete cookies directly in your browser:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

7. GDPR Privacy Rights (For EEA & UK Users)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you are entitled to specific data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Finejust.vip ensures you can exercise:

  • The Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • The Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request restriction of personal data processing.
  • The Right to Object to Processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.
  • The Right to Data Portability: You have the right to request transfer of collected data to another organization.

Note: Because Finejust.vip does not collect personal accounts, user profiles, or calculator submission logs, we do not retain identifiable records. If you submit an inquiry via our Contact Form, you may request deletion of your correspondence at any time by emailing info.finejust@gmail.com.

8. CCPA / CPRA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific privacy protections:

  • Right to Know: You may request disclosures of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected.
  • Right to Delete: You may request the deletion of personal information collected from you.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing: Finejust.vip does NOT sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for monetary or valuable consideration.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you, deny access, or alter tool performance because you exercised your CCPA/CPRA rights.

9. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Protecting the online privacy of young children is critically important. Finejust.vip does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union).

Our calculators and educational articles are intended for general audience learning and utility. If a parent or guardian believes that Finejust.vip has inadvertently received personally identifiable information from a child in our email records, please contact us immediately at info.finejust@gmail.com, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

10. External Links & Third-Party Services

Our website and educational guides may contain links to external resources, standards organizations (such as WHO or ISO), or academic citations. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of every external website you visit.

11. Data Security & Encryption Standards

We implement industry-standard SSL/TLS (Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security) cryptographic protocols across all URLs at https://finejust.vip. This ensures that all communication between your web browser and our CDN edge nodes remains encrypted, authenticated, and secure against eavesdropping.
