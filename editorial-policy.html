Editorial Policy & Standards

Our commitment to mathematical rigor, human expertise, transparent fact-checking, and continuous auditing.

Our Core Editorial Principles
  • Mathematical Verification: Every formula is verified against published academic and institutional standards (WHO, ISO, IEEE, statutory tax codes).
  • Human Review Board: All educational guides and tool interfaces are written, fact-checked, and reviewed by human domain specialists.
  • Zero Sponsored Bias: Commercial advertisers have zero influence on calculation algorithms, educational conclusions, or mathematical results.
  • Open Correction Policy: Rapid 24–48 hour resolution protocol for any identified calculation or typographical discrepancies.

1. Our Editorial Mission

At Finejust.vip (accessible at https://finejust.vip), our mission is to make mathematical, financial, technical, and unit calculations accessible, accurate, and completely free for a global audience.

We believe that online tools should empower students, developers, researchers, small business owners, and everyday consumers without compromising data privacy, loading speed, or mathematical integrity. This Editorial Policy defines our standards for content creation, formula derivation, peer review, and transparent corrections.

2. Adherence to Google E-E-A-T Guidelines

We design all calculators, unit converters, and educational blog guides in strict alignment with Google's Search Quality Evaluator Guidelines for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness (E-E-A-T):

  • Experience: Our team tests tools across real-world workflows (e.g. personal finance planning, software development, academic coursework, and international travel).
  • Expertise: Content is authored and audited by contributors with formal academic and professional backgrounds in mathematics, quantitative finance, cybersecurity, and computer science. Learn more on our Author Bio Page.
  • Authoritativeness: We cite primary standards organizations, including the World Health Organization (WHO), the International Organization for Standardization (ISO), the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE), and official national revenue departments.
  • Trustworthiness: We operate with 100% transparent client-side computing (zero data tracking), clear disclaimers, accessible contact channels, and open correction logs.

3. Mathematical & Algorithmic Verification Process

Before any interactive tool or educational guide is published on Finejust.vip, it must pass a rigorous Three-Tier Verification Lifecycle:

STAGE 1: FORMULA DERIVATION

Deriving equations from primary academic and statutory literature (e.g. reducing balance loan math, Shannon entropy, Quetelet body mass index).

STAGE 2: UNIT TEST AUDITING

Automated unit testing with boundary cases (e.g. zero values, negative numbers, extreme decimal precision, leap-year cycles).

STAGE 3: PEER EDITORIAL REVIEW

Human editorial review for clarity, accessibility, visual diagrams, and practical step-by-step examples.

4. Human Authorship & Review Board

Every article and technical guide published under the Finejust Editorial Team byline is created by human writers and reviewed by domain specialists.

Our contributors hold verifiable qualifications across quantitative analysis, web standards, and software development. We do not publish unvetted freelance submissions or generic content farm material.

5. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Transparency Policy

While Finejust.vip utilizes modern development technologies to enhance engineering productivity, our editorial policy regarding AI is unambiguous:

  • Zero Unverified AI Content: We strictly prohibit publishing raw, unverified AI-generated text or formulas.
  • Human Formula Validation: All mathematical algorithms, rate calculations, and code snippets are explicitly validated by human engineers against established mathematical theorems.
  • Originality & Style: All educational guides must meet strict original research, clarity, and readability benchmarks (Grade 6–8 easy English).

6. Independence & Commercial Integrity

Finejust.vip is funded through third-party display advertisements (Google AdSense). To maintain total editorial integrity:

  • Advertisers have zero access to or influence over our editorial board, calculation logic, formula choices, or educational conclusions.
  • We do not accept paid reviews, sponsored tool placements, or pay-to-win calculation rankings.
  • Our tools deliver objectively neutral mathematical results regardless of commercial partnerships.

7. Fact-Checking & Regulatory Tracking Schedule

Financial, tax, and technological standards evolve over time. Our editorial team conducts scheduled monthly audits to review:

  • Tax Slabs & Regulations: Tracking official updates from tax authorities (such as Indian GST Council notifications).
  • Forex & Banking Parameters: Verifying standard interbank quoting benchmarks and amortization conventions.
  • Cryptographic & Web Standards: Updating password entropy standards and QR matrix specifications in accordance with NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology) and W3C recommendations.

8. Corrections, Transparency & Community Feedback

We welcome and actively encourage feedback from our global community of users, teachers, students, and engineers. If you spot a formula bug, typographical typo, or outdated citation:

  1. Submit the issue via our Contact Form or email info.finejust@gmail.com.
  2. Our engineering team will reproduce the calculation within 24 to 48 business hours.
  3. If a correction is warranted, the code or article will be updated immediately, and a revision notice will be logged.
